# L'accord de l'adjectif qualificatif

## General rule

A descriptive adjective (`adjectif qualificatif`) **agrees in gender and number** with the noun it describes. This is true whether the adjective stands next to the noun or comes after the verb `être`.

- un garçon **grand** → une fille **grande**
- un livre **intéressant** → des livres **intéressants**
- Ma sœur est **contente**. (My sister is happy.)

## Feminine forms

Usually, add **-e** to the masculine form.

| Masculine | Feminine | Note |
|---|---|---|
| grand | grande | add -e, the final consonant is now pronounced |
| petit | petite | add -e |
| content | contente | add -e |
| jeune | jeune | already ends in -e: no change |
| rouge | rouge | already ends in -e: no change |

Some groups have their own rules:

| Masculine ending | Feminine ending | Masculine → feminine |
|---|---|---|
| -eux | -euse | heureux → heureuse |
| -if | -ive | sportif → sportive |
| -er | -ère | cher → chère, premier → première |
| -el | -elle | naturel → naturelle |
| -on, -ien | -onne, -ienne | bon → bonne, ancien → ancienne |

Some common adjectives have irregular feminine forms:

| Masculine | Feminine |
|---|---|
| beau | belle |
| nouveau | nouvelle |
| vieux | vieille |
| blanc | blanche |

## Plural forms

Usually, add **-s**.

- un petit chat → des petits chats
- une petite maison → des petites maisons

Other cases:

| Rule | Example |
|---|---|
| Already ends in **-s** or **-x**: no change | gros → gros, heureux → heureux |
| **-al** → **-aux** | normal → normaux |
| **-eau** → **-eaux** | beau → beaux, nouveau → nouveaux |

> **Tip:** say the word aloud. The feminine form often reveals a final consonant that the masculine form does not pronounce: `petit` /pə.ti/ → `petite` /pə.tit/.

## Agreement with several nouns

When one adjective describes both a masculine and a feminine noun, use the **masculine plural**.

- Paul et Marie sont **contents**. (Paul and Marie are happy.)
- Le garçon et la fille sont **français**. (The boy and the girl are French.)

## Invariable adjectives

Some colour names taken from nouns do **not** agree in gender or number, for example `marron` and `orange`.

- des chaussures **marron** (brown shoes)
- une chemise **orange** (an orange shirt)
