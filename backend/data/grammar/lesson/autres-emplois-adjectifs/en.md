# Autres emplois des adjectifs qualificatifs

## Beyond standing next to a noun

A descriptive adjective (`adjectif qualificatif`) does not only stand next to a noun. This lesson presents other common uses.

## 1. Attributive and predicative adjectives

| Role | Position | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **épithète** (attributive) | next to the noun | une **petite** maison (a small house) |
| **attribut** (predicative) | after a linking verb | La maison est **petite**. (The house is small.) |

Common linking verbs: `être`, `sembler`, `devenir`, `paraître`, `rester`.

- Elle semble **fatiguée**. (She seems tired.)
- Il devient **grand**. (He is getting tall.)
- Nous restons **calmes**. (We stay calm.)

In every position, the adjective still **agrees in gender and number** with the noun or the subject.

## 2. Adjectives used as nouns

After an article, some adjectives become nouns.

- Les **jeunes** aiment la musique. (Young people like music.)
- Un **vieux** lit le journal. (An old man is reading the newspaper.)
- Les **riches** et les **pauvres**. (The rich and the poor.)

These nouns still change with gender and number: `un jeune`, `une jeune`, `des jeunes`.

## 3. Adjectives used as adverbs

With some verbs, the adjective comes after the verb and works as an adverb. It is then **invariable**.

| Expression | Example |
|---|---|
| parler fort / bas | Il parle **fort**. (He speaks loudly.) |
| coûter cher | Ce pull coûte **cher**. (This sweater is expensive.) |
| travailler dur | Ils travaillent **dur**. (They work hard.) |
| chanter faux | Elle chante **faux**. (She sings out of tune.) |
| sentir bon / mauvais | Cette fleur sent **bon**. (This flower smells nice.) |

> **Tip:** compare `Cette robe est chère.` (predicative adjective, it agrees: chère) with `Cette robe coûte cher.` (used as an adverb: no change).

## 4. After quelque chose, rien, personne

After `quelque chose`, `rien`, `personne`, the adjective is introduced by **de** (or **d'** before a vowel) and stays masculine singular.

- Il y a quelque chose **d'**intéressant. (There is something interesting.)
- Je n'ai rien **de** nouveau à dire. (I have nothing new to say.)
- Il n'y a personne **de** malade. (There is nobody sick.)
