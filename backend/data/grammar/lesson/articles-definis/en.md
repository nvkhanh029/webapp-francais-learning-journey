# Les articles définis

## What is a definite article?

A definite article (`article défini`) comes before a noun to point to a person or thing that is **already identified**: the speaker and the listener both know who or what it is. It is also used to talk about a whole group in general, or about something unique.

## Forms

The definite article changes with the **gender** and **number** of the noun.

| Noun | Article | Before a consonant | Before a vowel / silent h |
|---|---|---|---|
| Masculine singular | **le** | le livre | l'ami, l'hôtel |
| Feminine singular | **la** | la table | l'école, l'heure |
| Plural | **les** | les livres | les amis, les écoles |

Before a vowel or a silent *h*, both **le** and **la** become **l'**, so you cannot see the gender of the noun. Learn the gender together with the word.

## Uses

1. **People or things that are known or already mentioned.**
   - Le livre est sur la table. (The book is on the table.)
   - Où est l'école ? (Where is the school?)
2. **Unique things.**
   - Le soleil brille. (The sun is shining.)
3. **General meaning, after verbs of preference** such as `aimer`, `adorer`, `détester`, `préférer`.
   - J'aime le café. (I like coffee.)
   - Elle déteste les araignées. (She hates spiders.)
4. **A habit on a day of the week.**
   - Le lundi, je travaille à la maison. (On Mondays, I work at home.)
5. **Languages and school subjects.**
   - J'apprends le français. (I am learning French.)

> **Tip:** if you can ask "which one?" or "who?" and the answer is already clear to both people, use the definite article.

## Contractions with à and de

When **le** or **les** comes after the preposition `à` or `de`, the two words merge into one.

| Preposition + article | Result | Example |
|---|---|---|
| à + le | **au** | Je vais **au** marché. (I am going to the market.) |
| à + les | **aux** | Je parle **aux** voisins. (I am talking to the neighbours.) |
| à + la | à la | Elle va **à la** gare. (She is going to the station.) |
| à + l' | à l' | Nous sommes **à l'**école. (We are at school.) |
| de + le | **du** | Le livre **du** professeur. (The teacher's book.) |
| de + les | **des** | Les clés **des** enfants. (The children's keys.) |
| de + la | de la | La porte **de la** maison. (The door of the house.) |
| de + l' | de l' | Le nom **de l'**étudiant. (The student's name.) |

> **Tip:** only **le** and **les** contract. With **la** and **l'** nothing changes: `à la`, `de la`, `à l'`, `de l'`.
