# Simple future formation

## Formula

**Infinitive + endings: -ai, -as, -a, -ons, -ez, -ont.**

For `-er` and second-group `-ir` verbs, keep the full infinitive:

- parler: je parlerai, tu parleras, il parlera, nous parlerons, vous parlerez, ils parleront.
- finir: je finirai, tu finiras, il finira, nous finirons, vous finirez, ils finiront.

For `-re` verbs, drop the final `e`: prendre -> prendr- -> je prendrai.

## Examples

- Je partirai demain. (I will leave tomorrow.)
- Tu réussiras. (You will succeed.)
- Elle travaillera à Paris. (She will work in Paris.)
- Nous mangerons ensemble. (We will eat together.)
- Vous comprendrez vite. (You will understand quickly.)
- Ils vendront la maison. (They will sell the house.)

## Pronunciation note

The `nous/vous` endings (`-ons, -ez`) are clearly pronounced /ɔ̃/, /e/, unlike the present tense.
