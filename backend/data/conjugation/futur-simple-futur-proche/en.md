# Simple future or near future?

## Near future formula

**present tense of aller + infinitive.**

- je vais parler, tu vas parler, il va parler, nous allons parler, vous allez parler, ils vont parler.

## Which one to use?

- **Futur proche**: firm plans, imminent events, present evidence. Clues: dans 5 minutes, ce soir, demain matin.
- **Futur simple**: distant predictions, promises, spontaneous decisions, formal writing. Clues: dans 10 ans, un jour, l'année prochaine.

## Contrast examples

- Je vais manger, j'ai faim ! (I am going to eat, I am hungry! — imminent.)
- Je mangerai plus tard. (I will eat later — uncertain.)
- Nous allons partir dans 5 minutes. (We are leaving in 5 minutes.)
- Un jour, je visiterai le Japon. (One day I will visit Japan.)
- Je vais aider Lan. (I am going to help Lan — already decided.)
- D'accord, je t'aiderai ! (OK, I will help you! — decided right now.)

## Note

In everyday speech, the futur proche is far more common for the near future.
