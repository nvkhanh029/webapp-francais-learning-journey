French uses the Latin alphabet of **26 letters**. The letter shapes are the same as in English, but the letter names and pronunciation differ quite a lot.

## Accents in French

Accents (*accents*) are placed above or below letters. An accent can change how a vowel is pronounced, separate two adjacent vowels, or simply distinguish two words that sound the same.

> **Note:** French accents are not tones like in Vietnamese. They do not change the pitch of a syllable; they change the sound of the vowel or the meaning of the word.

### é: *accent aigu*

**Acute accent.** Only appears on the letter e.

- Pronounced /e/, close to a clear "ay" sound.

**Examples**

- *café* /kafe/ — coffee
- *été* /ete/ — summer

### è à ù: *accent grave*

**Grave accent.** It changes the sound on è, and only distinguishes words on à and ù.

- è is pronounced /ɛ/, like the open "e" in *bed*.
- à and ù are pronounced like a and u.

**Examples**

- *mère* /mɛʁ/ — mother
- *à* (at, to) versus *a* (has)
- *où* (where) versus *ou* (or)

### â ê î ô û: *accent circonflexe*

**Circumflex.** It often marks a historical "s" that has dropped out of the word.

- ê is pronounced /ɛ/, ô is pronounced /o/, û is pronounced /y/.
- â and î barely change the sound.

**Examples**

- *fête* /fɛt/ — party, festival
- *forêt* /fɔ.ʁɛ/ — forest
- *hôtel* /o.tɛl/ — hotel

### ë ï ü: *tréma*

**Diaeresis.** It signals that the vowel is pronounced separately from the vowel before it.

- ü is rare: it signals that the letter u must be pronounced.

**Examples**

- *Noël* /nɔ.ɛl/ — Christmas
- *maïs* /ma.is/ — corn

### ç: *cédille*

**Cedilla.** Only used with the letter c.

- Pronounced /s/ before a, o, u.

**Examples**

- *français* /fʁɑ̃.sɛ/ — French
- *garçon* /ɡaʁ.sɔ̃/ — boy
- *leçon* /lə.sɔ̃/ — lesson

## Pronunciation and spelling notes

- **The letter `h` is always silent.** *hôtel* /o.tɛl/, *homme* /ɔm/
- **Final consonants are usually not pronounced.** *petit* /pə.ti/, *grand* /ɡʁɑ̃/
- **The letter `c`.** Pronounced /k/ before a, o, u and /s/ before e, i, y.
- **The letter `g`.** Pronounced /ɡ/ before a, o, u and /ʒ/ before e, i, y.

> **Tip:** when writing, an accent is part of the letter. *a* and *à*, *ou* and *où* have different meanings, so never drop the accents.

## Practice reading

1. *français* /fʁɑ̃.sɛ/ — French. The letter *ç* is pronounced /s/.
2. *élève* /e.lɛv/ — student. Tell *é* /e/ and *è* /ɛ/ apart.
3. *forêt* /fɔ.ʁɛ/ — forest. Circumflex on *ê*.
4. *Noël* /nɔ.ɛl/ — Christmas. The diaeresis separates o and e.
